What is NutriInfo?

🤖
AI-Powered Analysis

NutriInfo uses advanced artificial intelligence to analyze any food you type — portions, cooking methods, full meals — and instantly returns accurate, detailed nutrition data without any manual lookup.

📊
Complete Nutrition Data

Beyond basic calories — macros, fiber, 8 vitamins and minerals, a health score out of 10, smart badges, daily goal comparisons, and personalized diet tips for every food you search.

🗂
Category Browser

Explore 80+ curated foods across 7 categories — Fruits, Vegetables, Proteins, Grains, Dairy, Nuts, and Legumes. One tap sends any food straight into the analyzer.

📋
Nutrition Log

Every food you analyze is automatically saved to your session log. Track cumulative calories, protein, carbs, and fat with a live summary dashboard. Delete any entry individually.

🕐
Search History

Recent searches appear as quick-access chips below the search bar. Re-analyze with one tap, or remove entries with × — full control, zero clutter.

🔒
Privacy First

All session data stays in your browser only. NutriInfo never stores your search history on any external server. Close the tab and everything clears cleanly.

How it works

1️⃣
